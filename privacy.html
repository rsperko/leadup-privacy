LeadUp Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2025

Simple Summary

LeadUp does not collect any of your data. Everything stays on your phone. We can't see your events, and we don't want to.

Data Collection

LeadUp does not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Require accounts or login
  • Use analytics or tracking
  • Share data with third parties
  • Contain advertisements

Data Storage

All events and reminders are stored locally on your device using iOS Core Data. This data never leaves your device.

Notifications

LeadUp uses local notifications (scheduled on your device) to remind you of events. No data is sent to servers.
